# Youmanize

## Product

Youmanize is a local-first writing assistant for iOS and web. It learns a person’s writing style from material they choose, then helps them write, rewrite, and respond in that voice.

## Users

Individuals and teams who want AI-assisted writing without losing their own tone, vocabulary, rhythm, or intent.

## Personality

Human, clear, calm, precise, and privacy-conscious.

## Design principles

1. Voice ownership: the user controls their source material and profile.
2. Honest privacy: local storage and external processing are explained plainly.
3. Real product first: show actual workflows instead of generic AI claims.
4. Accessible by default: WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard support, visible focus, reduced motion, and readable contrast.
5. Quiet confidence: monochrome surfaces, purposeful hierarchy, restrained motion, and no decorative clutter.

## Avoid

Generic AI/SaaS language, invasive data practices, hidden limitations, exaggerated claims, and unnecessary interface chrome.
